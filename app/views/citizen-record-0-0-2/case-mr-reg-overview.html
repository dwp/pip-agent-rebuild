Request a mandatory reconsideration

You can ask for a mandatory reconsideration if any of the following apply:

  • you think we have made an error or missed important evidence
  • you disagree with the reasons for the decision
  • you want to have the decision looked at again

You should do this within one month of the date on your decision letter.

What will happen next

You can tell us the details of which parts of the decision you disagree with.

There'll be an opportunity to send additional evidence.

Someone will look at all the information again and make another decision. Your points could go up, down or remain the same.
