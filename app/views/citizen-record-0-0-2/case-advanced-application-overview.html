Reapplying for PIP (Rach)

To reapply for PIP, you will need to:

  • confirm your contact details
  • review what you said in your previous application
  • let us know whether your condition has improved or got worse
  • submit supporting evidence

We will then review your application and make a new decision.

Your points may go up, down or remain the same. This means your payments might change or stop completely if you no longer have enough points.
