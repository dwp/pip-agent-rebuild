Help with understanding your decision

Your PIP decision is made based on your ability to do everyday tasks and get around.

You are given points for 12 activities:

  • 10 for the daily living part of PIP
  • 2 for the mobility part of PIP

The outcome of your decision

Your decision was sent to you by post. It contains details of how many points you were given for each activity and why.

  • View the decision details
  • View the decision letter that was sent by post

How we made your decision

We looked at all the information available to us to understand whether you can carry out the 12 activities, and how much help you need to do them.

This includes:

  • information provided in your application
  • supporting evidence
  • information gathered at your health assessment, if you had one
  • [Application] [decision] summary
  • See what supporting evidence you have sent us

If you disagree with a decision

You, or someone who has the authority to act for you, can ask us to explain our decision.

  • Request a call back

You can also ask us to reconsider a decision. Do this within one month of the date on your decision letter.

  • Request a mandatory reconsideration
