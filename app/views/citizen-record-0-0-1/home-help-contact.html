Help and contact

If you need help with your Personal Independence Payment (PIP) application or understanding your decision, contact the PIP enquiry line.

If you need someone to help you

You can:

  • ask for them to be added to your call - you cannot do this if you use textphone
  • ask someone else to call on your behalf - you’ll need to be with them when they call
  • ask for a Welsh speaker - you’ll be given this option when the call begins

You may be able to get a home visit from a Department for Work and Pensions (DWP) officer to help you with your application. Ask the advisor when you call the PIP enquiry line.
